## AI-assisted Drug Discovery

My research focuses on developing artificial intelligence methods for computational drug discovery.

## Research Directions

### 1. Compound–Protein Interaction Prediction

Developing deep learning models for predicting molecular interactions using:

- Transformer
- Graph neural networks
- Protein language models


### 2. ADMET Prediction

Developing computational approaches for predicting:

- Absorption
- Distribution
- Metabolism
- Excretion
- Toxicity


### 3. Multimodal Biomedical AI

Integrating:

- Molecular information
- Protein sequences
- Biological context
- Omics data

for precision drug discovery.
