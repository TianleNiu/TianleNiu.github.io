I am Tianle Niu, a Ph.D. student in Biology at the FIST.
My research focuses on artificial intelligence-assisted drug discovery and computational pharmacology.

My current research interests include:

- Compound–Protein Interaction (CPI) prediction
- Drug–Target Interaction (DTI) prediction
- Deep learning for drug discovery
- ADMET prediction
- Explainable AI for biomedical research


## Research Interests

My research aims to develop computational approaches for understanding molecular interactions and accelerating drug discovery.

I am particularly interested in integrating:

- Molecular representation learning
- Protein sequence modeling
- Transformer-based models
- Graph neural networks
- Multi-modal biological data
