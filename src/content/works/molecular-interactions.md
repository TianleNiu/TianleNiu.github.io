This concept explores how a model might represent interactions between a molecule and a binding environment using the geometry of their local contacts.

## Research question

Which spatial features remain informative when the training and evaluation sets contain different molecular families? A useful evaluation would compare geometry-aware representations against simple contact baselines and make the data split explicit.
